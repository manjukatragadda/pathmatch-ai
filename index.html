GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations help you make better decisions.

Does PathMatch AI guarantee an internship?

No. It is designed to help you explore relevant paths. Selection depends on the employer, eligibility, your application, and interview performance.

Are these live internship vacancies?

No. The current page is a prototype and displays sample role suggestions, not verified live vacancies. Always check official employer websites for current openings.

Is this a real AI-powered product?

This version is a front-end demo with rule-based sample recommendations. A production AI matcher would need a verified internship dataset, matching logic or model, privacy safeguards, and ongoing testing.

Is my profile stored?

No. This demo processes selections locally in your browser and does not transmit or store your profile.

How should I use the skill-gap suggestions?

Treat them as a starting point. Check multiple real internship descriptions for the role you want, then prioritize the skills that appear repeatedly.
